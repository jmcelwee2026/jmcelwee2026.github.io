# Joshua McElwee

Personal site of Joshua McElwee — https://joshuamcelwee.com

A static portfolio bringing writing, research, and projects together under Joshua McElwee’s name. The main navigation is Home, Writing, Research, Projects, and About. Existing article URLs remain available.

## Published content

- `/writing/` lists all published papers, public comments, and the discussion series. The existing `/writing/#series` link opens the visible discussion section.
- `/research/` collects papers and public comments.
- `/research/faa-2026-8614-question-c/` is the landing page for the August 2026 public comment originally published under Mulapin Research Group. The original PDF is preserved byte-for-byte at `/research/Mulapin-Public-Comment-FAA-2026-8614-Question-C.pdf`, with its original filename and attribution. Migration does not revise the publication or independently establish its filing status.
- `/projects/` links to the public whiteboard conversation and technical guide.
- `/about/` retains the professional and personal pages, credentials, and contact information. `/about/#contact` opens the professional contact section.
- `/card/` is the interactive two-sided business card. It links to the main site and exposes only the public email, phone number, and LinkedIn profile approved for the card.

## Maintenance

Pages are plain HTML with local assets and inline styles. Keep `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` aligned with published pages. Preserve original publication files, article bodies, citations, and existing URLs when updating navigation. Local research files that have not been released are not part of this site.
