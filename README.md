# Joshua McElwee

Personal site of Joshua McElwee — https://joshuamcelwee.com

A static portfolio collecting Joshua McElwee’s work under his own name. The original main navigation is Home, Writing, and About. The homepage retains its simple name-and-credentials layout. Existing article URLs remain available.

## Published content

- `/writing/` retains the Papers & Commentary and Discussion Series tabs. Papers & Commentary contains the two research papers and the migrated FAA public comment; Discussion Series contains the two AI pieces and their existing introduction. `/writing/#series` opens Discussion Series, and `/writing/#public-comments` opens Papers & Commentary at the comment.
- `/writing/faa-2026-8614-question-c/` is the landing page for the August 2026 public comment originally published under Mulapin Research Group. The original PDF is preserved byte-for-byte at `/research/Mulapin-Public-Comment-FAA-2026-8614-Question-C.pdf`, with its original filename and attribution. Migration does not revise the publication or independently establish its filing status.
- `/research/`, `/projects/`, and `/research/faa-2026-8614-question-c/` are compatibility redirects to Writing, Discussion Series, and the comment’s current landing page. They preserve existing external links without adding separate site sections.
- `/about/` retains the professional and personal pages, credentials, and contact information. `/about/#contact` opens the professional contact section.
- `/card/` is the interactive two-sided business card. It links to the main site and exposes only the public email, phone number, and LinkedIn profile approved for the card.

## Maintenance

Pages are plain HTML with local assets and inline styles. Keep `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` aligned with published pages. Preserve original publication files, article bodies, citations, and existing URLs when updating navigation. Local research files that have not been released are not part of this site.
