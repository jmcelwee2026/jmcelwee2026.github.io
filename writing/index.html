AI Architecture · Access Modes · Verification

An ongoing series of whiteboard working sessions on AI system architecture — hosted assistant interfaces, agentic coding environments, and programmatic access through APIs — among a trusted circle of law students and practicing attorneys. The sessions run on shared ideas rather than instruction: each discussion tests and sharpens every participant's working methods, toward a stable common vocabulary in an area where the discourse is otherwise vendor marketing or vague caution.

A fixed whiteboard framework and a supporting 22-page technical reference keep the conversations grounded — the three access modes, the distinction between intelligence and automation, and verification duties that scale with consequence — so what the group works out in one session holds steady into the next.
